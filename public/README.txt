Profile Picture Instructions:
=============================
To display your personal profile photo on the portfolio:
1. Save your photo as `profile.jpg` (or PNG/WebP) in this `public/` folder.
   File path: `c:\Users\1567\Downloads\Shivani Portfolio\public\profile.jpg`
2. The website will automatically detect it and render it in the glowing animated hero avatar!
3. You can also test/preview any photo instantly by clicking "Add / Preview Photo" right in your browser.
